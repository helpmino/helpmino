# Helpmino

**English** | [Čeština](README.cs.md)

Helpmino is a project that offers simple, practical rules for a healthy lifestyle, connecting nutrition, movement, and the biology of the human body.

The goal is to build a community where people can share their experiences, discuss individual rules, and discover effective ways to improve their health and lifestyle together.

The project is open to everyone interested in a healthy lifestyle. It focuses on science-based information, practical advice, and mutual support among community members.

You can find more information and the individual rules in the project content, where the rules are sorted by topic and come with visual infographics and detailed biological explanations.

For inspiration, start with the [walk-after-meal rule](content/en/walk/rule-walk-after-meal.md), which shows how a short walk after eating can improve your metabolism and overall health.

## Contributing

If you want to contribute to Helpmino, open a pull request on GitHub. A contribution can be a new rule, an improvement to an existing rule, a bug fix, or an addition to a biological explanation.

Before you contribute, read the existing content and follow the established format of rules and infographics to keep the project consistent and of high quality.

Thank you for your interest in Helpmino. We look forward to your contributions!
